Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Controller

The controller for the processing of your personal data on this website is Mag. Lydia Valant (Orniello), Fischeraustraße 57, 8051 Graz, Austria, email: contact@orniello.com. You will find further details in the legal notice.

In short

I only process personal data where this is necessary to run this website and to answer your enquiries. This website does not set cookies, uses no web analytics or tracking, and does not embed advertising or content from third-party providers.

Hosting and server log files

This website is operated by an external hosting provider acting on my behalf. When you visit the site, the server processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, the date and time of access, the page requested, the amount of data transferred, and information about your browser and operating system. This is necessary to deliver the website and to keep it secure. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the secure and error-free operation of the website). The data is stored only for as long as this purpose requires.

Fonts

The fonts used on this website (Cormorant Garamond and Jost) are loaded from this server. No connection to Google or any other provider is established.

Language setting (local storage)

If you switch the language of the website, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so that the site appears in the same language on your next visit. This is only done when you actively choose a language. The information stays on your device and is not transmitted to me or to third parties. No cookies are set. You can delete the entry at any time in your browser settings. The storage is technically necessary to provide the language setting you asked for (§ 165 (3) TKG 2021).

Contact form and email

If you write to me via the contact form, I process the data you enter (name, email address, subject, message) in order to answer your enquiry. The form is provided by the service Formspree (Formspree, Inc., USA): your input is transmitted to Formspree, processed there and forwarded to me by email. Formspree also processes technical data such as your IP address. This involves a transfer of data to the USA; according to its own information, Formspree relies on standard contractual clauses for transfers from the EU.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your enquiry is aimed at a contract or a commission, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering enquiries). I delete your enquiry once it has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply. How long Formspree keeps data is governed by Formspree's own privacy policy.

Using the form is voluntary. Without name, email address and message I cannot answer you. Alternatively, you can write to me directly by email; in that case I process your email address and the contents of your message solely to answer it.

External links

This website contains links to external sites, for example Instagram, Facebook and Etsy. No plug-ins or embedded content from these providers are used: merely visiting my website does not transmit any data to them. Only when you click a link do you leave my website, and the privacy policy of the respective provider applies.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and the right to object (Art. 21). Simply contact contact@orniello.com. There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

If you believe that the processing of your data violates data protection law, you can also lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority: Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, Austria, email: dsb@dsb.gv.at, www.dsb.gv.at.
