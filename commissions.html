Custom Work

Commissions

Custom pieces · Alterations · Casting

You want a commission of something you saw in a painting, found in a museum or designed yourself?

You want something from my shop but in another colour, size or length?

Write to me! A lot of things are possible to be (re)created or altered. I can't promise that I can do it, but I'll try my best to realize your wishes!

Please note that as a lot of my pieces are unique they cannot be reproduced exactly. I collect materials for my works all over the world, including vintage ones, so some of the materials might not be available at short notice or a second time.

I want the same closure as the original / I want sterling silver hooks instead, can you change it?

For the most part, yes! Nearly all necklaces and bracelets can be made with hooks or ribbons instead of lobster clasps or locks. I can also easily change most earrings. These alterations are free of charge unless the material costs are higher.

You want something cast in bronze or silver?

Write to me! For bronze-cast items, I need several hours to carve the design in special wax, then make a mold and cast it at ~1100 °C. Therefore, I can offer a better price if I can re-use the design for other pieces or if you want a series. The waiting time for custom bronze pieces depends on the size and the quantity due to the casting process.

I cast in sand; complicated or multiplied casts are brought to a specialised caster and take a longer waiting time. Silver is always cast by myself.

I don't melt old jewelry for new pieces.

You have an old piece you want to turn into something new or alter to your liking?

You can send it to me! I can use the parts you have – e.g. from an old necklace – and turn them into your historical piece of art, or alter the piece into something you can wear every day and be happy with again. I also fix broken strands or closures.

Please note that I'm not allowed to repair or restore any precious metal pieces due to Austrian trade regulations!

Prices and conditions

The price for an hour of work is €60, excluding the cost of materials. Alterations of items from the shop in terms of size, colour or closures are free of charge unless the material costs of the replacement (e.g. a different kind of beads) are higher.

Commissions and individually adapted pieces are excluded from the statutory right of withdrawal, as they are made specifically for you. Your statutory warranty rights remain unaffected.
