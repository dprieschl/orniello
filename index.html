The Maker

Archaeology, craft,
and living history.

I'm Lydia Valant, jewelry maker. At about 13 I made my first metal jewelry piece; in my early twenties I earned my first money selling them at medieval fairs. Now, my focus is on reenactment and the recreation of historical jewelry and accessories.

I get inspired by archaeological finds or historical paintings from all over Europe and beyond. My creations are direct copies of or highly inspired by historical examples, but also modern interpretations of historical pieces.

Get in touch Details & FAQ

Custom Pieces

Something you saw in a painting, found in a museum or designed yourself. A lot of things can be (re)created – I'll try my best.

Casting in Bronze & Silver

Designs carved in wax and cast at ~1100 °C. Better pricing if I can re-use the design or for a series. Silver is always cast by myself.

Alterations & Repairs

Size, colour, closures – most shop items can be adapted, free of charge unless the materials cost more. Old pieces can be turned into something new. No repairs of precious metal (Austrian trade regulations).
